ECS service auto scaling now supports high resolution 20-second metrics, improving scale-out response time from 363 to 86 seconds (76% faster) and total scaling time from 386 to 109 seconds (72% faster). Users can configure 20-second resolution for CPU and memory utilization across Fargate, Managed Instances, and EC2 in all commercial and GovCloud regions.
