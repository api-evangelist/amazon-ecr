Amazon Quick adds autonomous agents for continuous task automation with granular autonomy levels and multi-dataset analytics enabling natural language queries across Snowflake and relational databases without technical data preparation. The redesigned activity feed provides conversational updates with feedback and direct approvals, and Quick apps can be shared as public websites.
