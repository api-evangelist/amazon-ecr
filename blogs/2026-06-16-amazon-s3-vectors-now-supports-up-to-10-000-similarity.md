S3 Vectors increases the similarity search result limit to 10,000, a 100x increase from the previous limit, with results returned across multiple pages for immediate processing of the first page. Larger result sets support multi-stage retrieval pipelines with reranking, aggregations, and deduplication; the first 512 KB per query is free.
