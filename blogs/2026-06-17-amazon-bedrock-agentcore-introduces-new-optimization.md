AgentCore now surfaces failure, intent, and trajectory insights and recommends specific system prompt improvements grounded in observed failures. Batch evaluation tests recommendations against defined datasets and A/B testing confirms improvements with statistical evidence. Insights are in preview across 13 regions; batch evaluations are generally available in 14 regions.
