HealthOmics now streams engine logs to CloudWatch in real time, giving visibility into workflow orchestration events, task scheduling details, import/export activity, and full stack traces on errors. Users can set CloudWatch alarms on log patterns and build dashboards for Nextflow, WDL, and CWL runs in all HealthOmics regions.
