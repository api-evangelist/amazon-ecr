title: "Amazon Connect Customer launches the ability to interrupt an agent with an urgent contact"

date: "2026-06-18"
feed_url: "https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/recent/feed/"
---
Amazon Connect now allows interrupting agents with urgent contacts, overriding usual routing so agents can receive urgent calls on personal extensions even while handling other calls. It also enables direct contact assignment to specific agents despite custom status settings, available in all regions where Amazon Connect operates.
