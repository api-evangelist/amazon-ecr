G7 instances accelerated by NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs deliver up to 4.6x AI inference performance and up to 2.1x graphics performance over G6. They feature up to 8 NVIDIA GPUs with 32GB memory each, custom Intel Xeon 6 processors, and 700 Gbps EFA networking, available in US East (Ohio) and US West (Oregon).
