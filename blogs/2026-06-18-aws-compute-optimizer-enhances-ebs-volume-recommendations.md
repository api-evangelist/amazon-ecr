Compute Optimizer now analyzes VolumeIOPSExceededCheck and VolumeThroughputExceededCheck CloudWatch metrics when recommending EBS rightsizing, helping balance cost with performance for bursty workloads. It is available in all regions except GovCloud and China, and the underlying metrics are free for Nitro-based EC2 instances.
