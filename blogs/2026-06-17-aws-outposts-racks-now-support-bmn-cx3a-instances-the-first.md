date: "2026-06-17"

Outposts racks now support bmn-cx3a instances with 5th Gen AMD EPYC processors and NVIDIA ConnectX-7 NICs delivering up to 800 Gbps of bare-metal accelerated network bandwidth. Two sizes (metal-32xl, metal-64xl) offer up to 256 cores, 1.5TB memory, and 16TB NVMe storage for real-time market data, analytics, and 5G applications.
