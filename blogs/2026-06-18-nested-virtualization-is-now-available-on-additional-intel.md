Nested virtualization now supports additional Intel platforms (C7i, R7i, M7i, C8id, R8id, M8id, C7i-flex, M7i-flex, I7i, C8i-flex, R8i-flex, M8i-flex, X8i) and US GovCloud regions. Users can run KVM or Hyper-V on EC2 for mobile emulators, vehicle hardware simulation, and Windows Subsystem for Linux.
