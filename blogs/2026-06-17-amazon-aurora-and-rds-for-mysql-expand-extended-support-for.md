Aurora MySQL version 2 and RDS for MySQL 5.7 receive Extended Support through June 30, 2029, extended from February 28, 2027. Customers get security patches for critical and high CVEs and bug fixes for critical operational issues with no price increase, and upgrade paths to MySQL 8.0/8.4 via Blue/Green Deployments.
