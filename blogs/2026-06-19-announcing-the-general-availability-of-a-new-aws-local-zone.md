AWS introduced a new Local Zone in Hanoi, Vietnam, extending infrastructure closer to end users with single-digit millisecond latency. It supports Amazon S3 and EBS Local Snapshots for data residency compliance, plus EC2 instances (C7i, M7i, R7i), ECS, EKS, VPC, Direct Connect, and Application Load Balancer. Users can enable the zone from the AWS Global View or via the ModifyAvailabilityZoneGroup API.
