AWS Graviton5-based M9g instances deliver up to 30% performance improvement and up to 23% price/performance improvement for on-demand pricing over Graviton4 equivalents. New 24xlarge and 48xlarge sizes offer up to 192 vCPU, 100Gbps networking, and 72Gbps EBS bandwidth, available in US East, US West (Oregon), and Europe (Frankfurt).
