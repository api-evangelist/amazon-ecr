Intelligent Rebalancing is now available on all existing MSK Provisioned clusters with Express brokers at no additional cost, optimally rebalancing Kafka resources and performing operations up to 180 times faster than Standard brokers. Clusters are continuously monitored for resource imbalance and scaled without affecting client availability.
