The all-MiniLM-L12-v2 model from Sentence Transformers is now available on SageMaker JumpStart, mapping sentences and paragraphs to a 384-dimensional dense vector space for semantic search, clustering, and similarity. Its compact architecture delivers fast inference and deploys via SageMaker Studio or the Python SDK.
